## Model Legend

- **Thermo-electric + Maxwell (thmagel)**: Coupled thermal, electrical and magnetic simulation
- **Elasticity (elas)**: Mechanical stress and deformation analysis
- **Commissioning (commi)**: Commissioning simulations
- **Thermo-electric (thelec)**: Thermal-electrical coupling
- **Thermo-magnetic (thmag)**: Thermal-magnetic coupling

## Naming Conventions

- **M8, M9, M10**: Housing/magnet version numbers
- **M8Bitters, M9Bitters, M10Bitters**: Bitter coils in M8, M9, M10 housings respectively
- **M10_**: Configuration on M10 site
- **M19XXXXXX**: Magnet IDs (e.g., M19020601, M19061901, M19071101)
- **2D/3D/Axi**: Simulation dimension (2D, 3D, or Axisymmetric)
- **CG**: Continuous Galerkin method
- **HDG**: Hybridizable Discontinuous Galerkin method
- **CFPDES**: Specific finite element solver
- **frans**: Studies performed in collaboration with HMFL Nijmegen (F. Wijnen)
- **Be/Bi**: Material variants (Beryllium/Bismuth)
- **18MW**: New Bitter coil design supporting up to 18 Megawatt power
- **XXkA**: Current level in kiloamperes
